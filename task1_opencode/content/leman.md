## What I think about AI in software engineering today

AI has changed software engineering as a whole. It has created some good and bad effects.
Menial and boring setup and tasks can be done very quickly, but it has also had an effect on Job sector.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years

I think software engineering will shift from just doing code line by line towards designing systems, and those who are ahead
on developing entire system by using AI tools will be ahead than others.
Also, no more wasted time in writing boilerplate codes. AI can setup these easily as well as automatically process commands from building an app to shipment.

## My hot take on AI

AI won't replace good software developers. Better developers are who that can use AI tools better.

## What I think about AI in software engineering today
I think AI is making engineers' lives both easy and hard. It makes their work easier by doing a lot of tasks for them, but at the same time, it pushes engineers to keep learning and improving their skills to keep up with new technologies.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
I think AI will be doing a lot more coding in the future, and engineers will spend more time figuring out what to build. Keeping up with AI and new technologies will become an important part of being a software engineer.

## My hot take on AI
AI is making coding easier, but being a software engineer harder!

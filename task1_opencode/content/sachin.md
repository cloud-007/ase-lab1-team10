## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI is fantastic for replacing the boring parts of setup and generating standard testing scripts. However, relying on it blindly is incredibly dangerous because it frequently outputs code with hidden logic vulnerabilities. It is an amplifier for engineers who already know what they are doing, but a trap for beginners.


## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
In next five to ten years, manually writing raw code blocks will be a legacy workflow. Software engineering will mature into a discipline of strict specification writing, prompt containment boundary design, and automated continuous validation loops. We will be architects and judges of code, not typists.


## My hot take on AI

AI shouldn't replace our critical thinking; it should augment it. Outsourcing our logic to algorithms breeds intellectual laziness.
